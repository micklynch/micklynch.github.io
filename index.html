01about

Engineer by training, builder by instinct.

I've led large-scale transformations inside health systems, started a company from scratch, and stayed close enough to the code to keep shipping open source. My graduate research was about building the datasets that teach computers human-like tasks, and that foundation still shapes how I think about AI.

The through-line is translation: taking something genuinely hard — a FHIR server, a clinical workflow, a research model — and turning it into a product a real person can use. Fewer demos that go nowhere; more systems in production where patients feel the difference.

02focus

Three problems I keep coming back to.

a.

Agents that cooperate

Multi-agent systems that reason over real clinical context, with orchestration, triage and FHIR tools wired together through open protocols.

b.

Clinical data, made legible

Interfaces and MCP servers that let care teams and patients question health records in plain language instead of query syntax.

c.

Product at scale

Taking AI from pilot to production across large health systems: driving adoption, cutting clinical workload, owning the roadmap.
