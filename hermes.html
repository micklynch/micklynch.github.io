Hermes Personal Assistant

A private, self-hosted personal assistant application that manages email, calendar, documents, and task automation for a single user, the owner-operator.

Who it's for

Single user. The application only ever accesses the account of the person who runs it.

What it does

Reads & sends email, manages calendar events, and touches Drive/Docs/Sheets to automate the owner's workflows.

Data handling

No selling, no sharing, no ads. Data stays within the owner's own devices and accounts.
