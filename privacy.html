Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-07 · Hermes Personal Assistant

Overview

Hermes Personal Assistant ("the app") is a self-hosted productivity tool operated by Michael Lynch for their own personal use. This policy describes how the app handles the data it can access through your Google account. It is kept as simple as the app is.

Data the app accesses

When authorized, the app may access:

  • Gmail — to read, draft, and send email on the owner's behalf.
  • Google Calendar — to read and create events.
  • Google Drive, Docs, and Sheets — to read and create documents.
  • Google Contacts — to read contact information.

Because the app is single-user, this is always the personal data of the person who operates it (the account owner), never the data of third parties.

How data is used

Data is used only to perform the tasks the owner asks it to do — for example, checking mail, adding a calendar event, or summarizing a document. It is never used for advertising, sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Where data is stored

The app runs on hardware and servers controlled by the owner (a self-hosted environment). OAuth tokens and cached data are stored on those systems. No data is sent to or stored by the app developer's own cloud, and no data leaves the owner's environment except where a task itself requires interacting with your Google account.

Access & retention

Data is kept only for as long as needed to complete the owner's current tasks. Unneeded local caches and tokens can be deleted by the owner at any time, and access to Google data can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party access.

Data deletion

To delete data held by the app, the owner removes the app's cached files and revokes access from their Google Account. Because the app stores nothing centrally, this fully erases the data by design.

Security

Access to Google data is controlled by OAuth scopes limited to what the app needs. Tokens are stored only on the owner's own systems. The owner is the sole administrator and only authorized user.
